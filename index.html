Frequently Asked Questions

What if I just need one thing built?

That's what the Project Build is for. Fixed scope, fixed price. We build it, hand it over, done. If you want to keep working together after that, we can move to a retainer.

How fast do you deliver?

Project builds typically ship in 2-5 weeks depending on scope. For retainer work, most requests turn around within 48 hours. Either way, you'll have full visibility the entire time.

What's the onboarding process like?

Once you sign up, you'll get instant access to your own private channel in our Slack. From there, you can submit your first request and we'll guide you through anything you need—super simple and personalized.

Who's actually doing the work?

Cam and Erick. Two senior creatives with deep experience in branding, product design, development, and digital. We've built for startups, agencies, web3 protocols, and some of the most recognizable brands out there. No outsourcing, no juniors.

What can you actually build?

Brands, websites, web apps, landing pages, design systems, pitch decks, product UI. We handle strategy, design, and development. If it lives on a screen and needs to look great, we can build it.

Can I pause or cancel the retainer?

Yes. Pause anytime and we'll bank your remaining days. Cancel anytime. No long-term contracts.

What's your tech stack?

Design in Figma. Build in Next.js, Webflow, Framer, or whatever fits your stack. We're tool-agnostic and pick what's right for the project.

Do you handle development too?

Yes. We design and build. Websites, web apps, landing pages. Clean, responsive, production-ready. Once it's done, we transfer ownership to you. No separate dev team, no handoff friction.

How do we communicate?

You get a private Slack channel. Send requests, feedback, files, Looms, whatever works for you. We're async-first but always responsive.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

We revise until you are. Collaboration is built into the process. You'll see work early and often, so there are no surprises at the end.

What don't you do?

We focus on digital. A few things outside our scope:

  • 3D modeling

  • Motion graphics and animation

  • Print production (magazines, packaging)

  • Native mobile app development (iOS/Android)

If it's a grey area, just ask. We'll figure it out.

Are there any refunds?

Because of the nature of creative work and the time involved, we don't offer refunds. But we're committed to making sure you love what you get.
